What makes it different

  • Honest difficulty. Each puzzle is solved by a logic engine before you see it. Easy needs only singles; Expert really does need an X-Wing, an XY-Wing or a Swordfish. Hard isn’t just “more empty squares”.
  • Hints that explain. A hint first shows you where to look, without giving the answer away. Ask again and it names the technique, explains the reasoning in plain words and marks the squares involved. Hints are free and unlimited.
  • Learn the techniques. Thirteen short lessons, from Hidden Single to Swordfish, each shown on a real board at the exact moment it’s needed. Then try it yourself on that board.
  • Three ways to play. Classic; Killer, with dashed cages and their sums; and Diagonal, where both long diagonals hold 1 to 9 too. Four levels each.
  • A daily puzzle, the same for everyone, getting harder through the week. Keep a streak, or play missed days from the calendar.
  • Mistakes don’t end your game. Wrong numbers can turn red as you go. There’s no three-strikes rule unless you switch it on, and even then you can keep going.
  • Made for solving. Pencil notes, fill all notes, hold a number to lock it, unlimited undo, four pen colours, a night theme, and keyboard support on iPad.
