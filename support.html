Common questions

How do I play?

Fill the grid so that every row, every column and every 3×3 box holds the numbers 1 to 9 once each.

  • Killer adds dashed cages. The numbers in a cage add up to the small total in its corner and never repeat inside it.
  • Diagonal adds both long diagonals, which must hold 1 to 9 as well.

Tap a square, then a number. Or hold a number to lock it, then tap squares to place it. Turn on Notes to pencil in candidates; the ••• menu can fill in all notes for you.

Is there always exactly one answer?

Yes. Every puzzle is checked when it’s made: it has a single solution, reachable by logic alone, with no guessing. If you ever find one with two answers, please email us a screenshot.

How do hints work?

Hints are free and unlimited. The first tap shows you where to look without giving the number away. Tap Show me to see the technique’s name, the reasoning in plain words and the squares involved. If you’ve entered a wrong number, the hint points that out first. If no logical step can be found, it offers to fill in one square.

How does the daily puzzle work?

There’s one daily puzzle a day, the same for every player, and it gets harder through the week:

MON easy · TUE–WED medium · THU–FRI hard · SAT expert · SUN hard

Solve it on the day to keep your streak. Missed days can be played from the calendar on the Daily tab, but only the day’s own puzzle counts toward the streak.

Will three mistakes end my game?

No. By default wrong numbers just turn red. You can switch on Stop after three mistakes in Settings, and even then you can choose to keep going. Both checks can be turned off.

Is Ninefold free? What does Remove Ads do?

Everything in the app is free. A short ad can appear after you finish a puzzle, never while you’re solving, on pause or in a lesson. Missed daily puzzles in the archive open after an optional short video. Remove Ads (Settings → Ads) is a single purchase that switches both off for good. It’s shared with your Family Sharing group.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I restore it?

Open Settings → Ads → Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do my records and streak sync between devices?

Not yet. Ninefold has no account and keeps your games, records and streak on the device itself. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I stop personalised ads?

On your device open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and switch Ninefold off. Ads still appear after puzzles but are no longer personalised. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you can also change your choice from Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices inside the app.

Reporting a problem

The most useful report says what you tapped, what happened and what you expected. Your device model, iOS version and the puzzle type and level help too. Email programmingdash@gmail.com.
