Privacy Policy

Ninefold is a Sudoku app. This page says exactly what it keeps on your device, what leaves your device, and what it never collects.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · Applies to Ninefold for iPhone and iPad

The short version. There’s no account and no sign-in. Your games, records and streak stay on your device and are never uploaded. The only part of Ninefold that goes online is advertising, described in full below, and buying Remove Ads switches it off. There’s no analytics SDK, and we never sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app’s own storage. None of it is sent anywhere, and the developer has no access to it.

WhatWhy it exists
Games in progressOne unfinished game per puzzle type, plus unfinished daily puzzles: your numbers, notes, time, mistakes and hints, so you can carry on later.
Records and streakThe puzzles you’ve finished (type, level, time, mistakes, hints, date) and the days you solved the daily puzzle, for the Record and Daily tabs.
Ready-made puzzlesA few puzzles generated in advance on the device, so a new game opens instantly. They contain no personal data.
SettingsPen colour, appearance, mistake checking, highlighting, clock and haptics.
Ad spacing and purchaseHow many puzzles you’ve finished and when the last ad was shown, used only to space ads out; and whether you’ve bought Remove Ads.

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no iCloud sync, so your records belong to the device they were made on.

Permissions the app asks for

Ninefold doesn’t ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications. The only permission it asks about is Tracking (the “Allow Ninefold to track” prompt), which exists for advertising and is explained below. It isn’t shown until you’ve finished your first puzzle. You can decline, and the whole app works the same.

Advertising

Ninefold is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. There are two kinds of ad: a short full-screen ad after you finish a puzzle, when you tap Next or Done, and an optional video you can choose to watch to open a missed daily puzzle from the archive. Ads never appear while you’re solving, when you pause, when you leave an unfinished puzzle, or in a lesson. Your first two puzzles have none, and there are at least two minutes between ads.

To choose and measure ads, the AdMob SDK collects:

  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, ads still appear but aren’t personalised.
  • Advertising data: which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them.
  • Approximate location at country or region level, inferred from your IP address. The app never asks for location permission.
  • Diagnostic data about how the advertising SDK is performing.

Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from partner apps. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app first asks for your consent through Google’s consent form. You can change that choice later from Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices inside the app.

To stop personalised ads anywhere: on your device open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and switch Ninefold off.

Remove Ads purchase

Remove Ads is an in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. Ninefold never sees your payment details; it only receives confirmation from the App Store that the purchase belongs to your Apple Account. Once it’s bought, the app stops starting the ad SDK, so none of the advertising data above is collected.

What the app does not collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses or passwords.
  • No analytics or tracking tools other than the advertising SDK described above.
  • Your games, records and settings are never uploaded to any server.
  • We never sell or share your data, and you’ll never get marketing messages from us.

Children

Ninefold is a general-audience app, not directed at children, and is rated 4+ on the App Store. Its advertising isn’t set up as child-directed.

Changes

If the app’s data behaviour changes, this page is updated in the same release and the date above changes with it.
